# Changelog

All notable changes to Hunches & Herrings. Versions follow `major.minor.patch`.

## 0.4.0 — unreleased

- **Eleven kinds of case:** a lone killer, two conspirators, the children acting together (to inherit, or to cut someone out of
  the will), a house where everyone hides a secret, a servant who is secretly the victim's child, an accident made to look like
  murder, the doctor's poison, the doctor's fatal blunder, an affair, a killer who struck the wrong person, and an impostor.
  Leave it as a surprise, or pick one on the title screen.
- **Accuse one, several or nobody:** tick everyone you think acted together, or declare it no murder at all. Naming only some of
  the guilty earns half credit.
- **Forty-one identifying facets:** handedness, boot size, scent, ink, gloves, hobnails, a stammer, a gold tooth, a sailor's
  knot, a fear of heights and many more. Each case uses a few, and some indicators turn out to have an innocent explanation.
- **Why the house is shut:** flood, snow, a fallen bridge, police orders, quarantine, the reading of the will, the tide, a
  strike or fog, each with its own rules about who can come and go.
- **Callers from outside (⌂):** the doctor, the constable, the vicar or the solicitor call at certain hours. Send for them
  when you need them. The visitors' book records their comings and goings.
- **Secrets and confrontations:** people with secrets lie about them. Find the proof among their things, then confront them.
- **Children of the house:** they can't be accused, never lie, and only ever tell you part of what they saw.
- **A rival detective (optional):** wanders the house, closets suspects, pockets evidence before you, and trades tips
  (not all of them true on the harder ranks). Their own verdict is revealed at the end.
- **Painted portraits** for most roles, matched to each character's hair and spectacles. The ink drawings remain as a fallback.
- **Suspects tab:** wraps instead of scrolling sideways, showing only the evidence you've found. **Dossier window:** a
  breakout window with everything known about everyone.
- **Journal rings:** click a word, or drag across words (even onto the next line), to ring just that part of an entry.
  Rings that overlap or touch merge into one. Rub rings out with the eraser, or right-click a word. The Circled tab gathers
  every ringed point on one page.
- **Map header:** the hour controls and the floor tabs each get their own line.
- Saves from 0.2 can't be resumed (the cases are built differently); replay them from their case number.

## 0.2.0

- **Your detective:** a name and gender. The household and the sergeant address you as "Detective {Surname}", "sir" or "ma'am".
- **Title screen:** a logo, ranks, case tuning (suspects, deadline, rescues, red herrings, a reliable sergeant), fixed
  or random case numbers, and recent saves.
- **Generated case names:** e.g. *The Case of the Crooked Candlestick*, *Murder at Ravensmere Hall*.
- **The journal:** a leather-bound book that a quill writes new entries into. It has page turns, tabs and red-ink
  circles.
- **Blueprint map:** walls, door swings, windows and furniture, plus a red crime scene with a chalk outline, your
  footprint trail and numbered stops, and the sergeant's badge.
- **Floor tabs:** glyphs for you, the sergeant, the murder floor and watched suspects.
- **Whereabouts by the hour:** known people are marked exactly. Everyone else is rough, by the household's routine.
- **Watch suspects:** circle up to three suspects to keep an eye on them on the plan.
- **Sergeant chat:** give orders and ask questions in plain English, or use the quick buttons.
- **Installer:** a per-user Inno Setup installer (`publish.bat`), the same routine as Pixel8.
- **Logo and icon:** an HH monogram on parchment.
