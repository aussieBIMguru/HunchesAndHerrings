# Hunches & Herrings v0.4.0

**Every case tells a different story now.** It isn't always one person killing one person: some cases have a pair of conspirators, some have a whole family in on it, and some have no murderer at all. Callers come and go from the house, the household keeps secrets, and a rival detective may be on the case alongside you.

## Eleven kinds of case

- **A lone killer.** The classic.
- **Two conspirators.** One strikes the blow; the other swears they were together all evening.
- **The children, together.** They acted to inherit, or to burn a new will that cut them out in favour of someone else.
- **A house of secrets.** Everyone is hiding something, and the motive is one of those secrets.
- **A secret heir.** A servant is the victim's unacknowledged child, and may be the killer or the reason for the killing.
- **No murder at all.** An accident that someone, for their own reasons, made look like murder.
- **The doctor's poison.**
- **The doctor's blunder.** A fatal dose given in error, then covered up.
- **An affair.** Either the victim or the spouse was straying.
- **The wrong victim.** The killer meant to kill somebody else.
- **An impostor.** Someone in the house isn't who they claim to be.

Cases are a surprise by default, but you can choose a kind on the title screen.

**Accusing:** tick everyone you believe acted together, or declare that there was no murder. Naming only some of the guilty earns half credit.

## Deeper investigations

- **41 identifying details.** Ink colour, hobnails, a stammer, a gold tooth, a sailor's knot, a fear of heights, scent, gloves, a limp and more. Each case draws on a few of them, and some evidence at the scene turns out to have an innocent explanation.
- **Why the house is shut.** It can be a flood, snow, a fallen bridge, police orders, quarantine, the reading of the will, the tide, a strike or fog. Each decides who can come and go today.
- **Callers from outside (⌂).** The doctor, constable, vicar or solicitor call at certain hours. You can send for them, and the visitors' book in the hall records their comings and goings last night.
- **Secrets and confrontations.** People with secrets lie about them. Find the proof among their things, then confront them, and they'll come clean about the secret, if not the crime.
- **Children of the house.** They can't be accused and never lie, but they only ever tell you part of what they saw.
- **A rival detective (optional).** They wander the house, keep suspects busy, pocket evidence before you find it, and trade tips. On harder ranks not all of those tips are true. Their own verdict is revealed at the end.

## A new look

- **Painted portraits.** 80 sepia portraits cover every role, and each character's hair and spectacles match their picture, so the faces are evidence too.
- **A reworked Suspects tab.** It now wraps instead of scrolling sideways, and shows only the evidence you've found.
- **The dossier.** A breakout window with a card for everyone: where they were placed last night, every detail and motive you've heard, secrets, and clashes. It stays open beside the game.
- **Rings in the journal.** Click a word, or drag across words, to ring just that part of an entry. Rings that overlap or touch merge into one; rub them out with the eraser or a right-click. The Circled tab gathers every ringed point together.
- **A tidier map header.** The hour controls and the floor tabs each have their own line, so nothing gets cut off.

## Notes

- Saves from 0.2 can't be resumed, because cases are now built differently. Replay them from their case number.
- A given case number produces a different mystery than it did in 0.2.
